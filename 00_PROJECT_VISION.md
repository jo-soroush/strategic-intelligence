# Strategic Intelligence Project — Project Vision

## 1. Project Vision

Build a **governed AI strategic intelligence system** that helps me prepare for important meetings and negotiations with company executives.

The system should turn scattered public information into a clear, evidence-backed **Strategic Meeting Intelligence Brief**.

**Better Intelligence → Better Preparation → Better Decisions**

---

## 2. Problem

Before meeting an executive, useful information is spread across many places:

- Company websites
- Public LinkedIn information
- News and announcements
- Reports
- Public case studies
- Projects
- Partnerships
- Events and conferences
- Interviews
- Articles
- Professional public information

Finding, checking, connecting, and understanding this information manually takes significant time.

I need to understand:

- What is happening in the company?
- What projects are they working on?
- What direction is the company moving in?
- Who is the executive?
- What are their professional priorities?
- What recent developments matter?
- Where may opportunities exist?
- What should I know before the meeting?
- What questions should I ask?

---

## 3. V1 User

V1 has **one primary user: me**.

I may use the system before:

- A job-related meeting
- A networking meeting
- A business negotiation
- A partnership discussion
- A sales or consulting conversation

V1 should be designed specifically around this use case.

---

## 4. Main V1 Use Case

I provide:

**Company + Executive + Meeting Goal**

I may also provide the public LinkedIn URLs for the company and executive.

Example:

**Company:** Capgemini Invent
**Executive:** Selected company executive
**Goal:** Explore a potential professional opportunity

The system researches relevant public information, verifies important findings, analyzes them, and prepares me for the meeting.

---

## 5. What V1 Should Deliver

### Company Intelligence

- Current and recent projects
- Public client projects and case studies
- AI / GenAI / Agentic AI initiatives
- New products and services
- Strategic priorities
- Partnerships
- Investments and acquisitions
- Hiring and expansion signals
- Important recent developments
- Relevant events and conferences
- Relevant public LinkedIn information

### Executive Intelligence

- Current professional role
- Area of responsibility
- Relevant professional background
- Public professional LinkedIn information
- Public posts and articles
- Interviews
- Talks and events
- Topics they discuss professionally
- Relevant professional priorities

### Strategic Analysis

The system should identify:

- What appears important to the company
- What appears important to the executive
- What recent developments matter
- What company projects reveal about its direction
- Where relevant opportunities may exist
- What I should understand before the meeting
- Useful topics to discuss
- Intelligent questions to ask
- Important assumptions and uncertainties to avoid

---

## 6. LinkedIn in V1

LinkedIn is an important **public intelligence source**.

V1 may use:

- Public company LinkedIn information
- Public executive professional information
- Relevant public posts
- Articles
- Events
- Job information
- Company announcements

The user may provide LinkedIn URLs as input.

**V1 will not implement automated LinkedIn scraping.**

LinkedIn information should be combined with other public sources such as official company websites, reports, news, case studies, interviews, and event pages.

---

## 7. Trust and Governance

The system must not present AI-generated assumptions as facts.

Important information should be evidence-backed.

V1 should clearly distinguish:

**FACT** — supported by evidence

**INFERENCE** — interpretation based on available evidence

**RECOMMENDATION** — suggested action or discussion point

V1 should also consider:

- Source provenance
- Publication date
- Information freshness
- Evidence quality
- Uncertainty
- Conflicting information
- Privacy
- Data minimization
- Basic auditability

If reliable evidence is not available, the system should say so instead of inventing an answer.

---

## 8. V1 Final Output

The main output is a:

# Strategic Meeting Intelligence Brief

It should quickly answer:

- Who am I meeting?
- What is happening inside their company?
- What projects and strategic initiatives matter?
- What appears important to this executive?
- What opportunities may be relevant?
- What should I discuss?
- What intelligent questions should I ask?
- What should I avoid assuming?
- What information is uncertain?
- What evidence supports the important findings?

The brief should be concise enough to use before a real meeting while allowing important findings to be traced back to their sources.

---

## 9. V1 Success Definition

V1 is successful if, before a real meeting, I can provide:

**Company + Executive + Goal**

and receive a trustworthy brief that makes me **meaningfully better prepared than normal manual research alone**.

The system should save research time without sacrificing evidence, transparency, or human judgment.

---

## 10. Future Vision

After V1 is proven useful for me, the product may later expand to:

- Consultants
- Business development professionals
- Sales teams
- Strategy teams
- Client intelligence
- Account intelligence
- Competitive intelligence
- Continuous monitoring and alerts

These are **not V1 requirements**.

V1 remains focused on one problem:

> **Help me enter an important executive meeting with better, evidence-backed strategic intelligence.**
