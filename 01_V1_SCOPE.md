# Strategic Intelligence Project — V1 Scope

## 1. V1 Goal

V1 has one clear purpose:

> **Help me prepare strategically for an important meeting or negotiation with a company executive.**

V1 must be useful in a real meeting, not only work as a technical demo.

Target development time:

**Maximum 3 weeks**

---

## 2. V1 Primary User

V1 has one primary user:

**Me**

Possible meeting types:

- Job opportunity
- Professional networking
- Business negotiation
- Partnership discussion
- Consulting conversation
- Sales/business opportunity

Other user types are outside V1.

---

# 3. Input

## Required

The user provides:

- **Company name**
- **Target executive name**
- **Meeting goal**

## Optional

The user may also provide:

- Company website
- Company LinkedIn URL
- Executive LinkedIn URL
- Extra meeting context

Example:

**Company:** Capgemini Invent
**Executive:** Selected executive
**Goal:** Explore an AI Strategy opportunity
**Extra Context:** Understand their current AI projects and where my experience may be relevant.

---

- Automation
- AI Governance
- Relevant emerging technologies

## 4.6 Products & Services

- Important current offerings
- New services
- AI-related services
- Offerings relevant to the meeting

## 4.7 Partnerships

Relevant partnerships such as:

- Technology companies
- Cloud providers
- AI companies

## 4.8 Investments & Acquisitions

Relevant recent:

Research relevant hiring activity to understand:

- Growing capabilities
- Skills being recruited

V1 researches the target executive's relevant public professional information.

## 5.1 Current Role & Responsibilities

Understand:

- Current position
- Business area
- Responsibilities
- Relevant leadership scope

## 5.2 Relevant Career Background

Only professional history useful for understanding the meeting context.

## 5.3 Current Professional Focus

Identify topics and business areas currently relevant to the executive.

## 5.4 Public LinkedIn Activity

Relevant publicly accessible professional information may include:

- Posts
- Articles
- Professional announcements
- Events
- Topics

No automated LinkedIn scraping is included in V1.

## 5.5 Articles & Publications

Relevant:

- Articles
- Reports
- Thought leadership
- Publications

## 5.6 Interviews & Podcasts

Relevant public professional appearances.

## 5.7 Events, Talks & Panels

Identify relevant:

- Conferences
- Talks
- Panels
- Seminars
- Public appearances

## 5.8 Projects & Initiatives

Identify publicly available projects or initiatives connected to the executive when evidence exists.

## 5.9 Repeated Topics

Identify professional topics the executive discusses repeatedly.

## 5.10 Recent Professional Activity

Prioritize recent and meeting-relevant information.

## 5.11 Connection to Company Strategy

Analyze how the executive's role and public activity connect to the company's wider direction.

## 5.12 Possible Priorities

The system may identify possible professional priorities.

These must be clearly labeled as:

**INFERENCE**

unless directly supported as factual statements.

# 6. Strategic Analysis

Research alone is not enough.

V1 must transform research into useful strategic intelligence.

## 6.1 What Matters Now?

Identify the most important current developments related to the company, executive, and meeting goal.

## 6.2 Company Direction

Analyze where the company appears to be:

- Investing
- Expanding
- Transforming
- Building capabilities
- Changing direction

## 6.3 Executive Priorities

Identify what appears professionally important to the executive.

Evidence-backed statements and AI inference must remain separate.

## 6.4 Project Meaning

Analyze what recent projects and case studies may reveal about:

- Company priorities
- Capabilities
- Client demand
- Strategic direction

## 6.5 Opportunity Areas

Identify areas where a relevant opportunity may exist based on the user's meeting goal.

## 6.6 My Relevance

Connect relevant evidence and opportunities to the user's supplied context and meeting goal.

The system should not invent user experience or capabilities.

## 6.7 Meeting Topics

Recommend useful topics worth discussing.

## 6.8 Smart Questions

Generate specific questions based on actual research and evidence.

Avoid generic interview or networking questions when stronger evidence-backed questions are possible.

## 6.9 Risks / Do Not Assume

Identify:

- Weak assumptions
- Unsupported conclusions
- Sensitive uncertainties
- Information that should not be presented as fact

## 6.10 Knowledge Gaps

Clearly identify important information that could not be reliably verified.

---

# 7. V1 Governance

Governance is a real system requirement in V1.

It is not only documentation.

## 7.1 Source Provenance

Important factual findings should retain:

- Source
- URL/reference
- Publication date when available
- Retrieval date
- Relevant evidence

## 7.2 Evidence Requirement

Important factual claims require supporting evidence.

Unsupported claims must not silently appear as facts.

## 7.3 Fact / Inference / Recommendation

The system must distinguish:

### FACT

Supported by evidence.

### INFERENCE

AI interpretation based on available evidence.

### RECOMMENDATION

Suggested action, question, or discussion point.

These categories must not be silently mixed.

## 7.4 Freshness

The system should consider:

- Publication date
- Retrieval date
- Information age
- More recent conflicting information

Old information should not automatically be treated as current.

## 7.5 Cross-Source Verification

Important claims should be checked against additional reliable sources when practical and valuable.

## 7.6 Conflict Detection

When credible sources conflict, the system should expose the conflict instead of silently selecting one version.

## 7.7 Uncertainty & Abstention

When evidence is insufficient, the system should communicate this clearly.

Example:

**Insufficient Evidence**

Guessing is not an acceptable replacement for missing evidence.

## 7.8 Source Quality

Prefer stronger sources when possible.

Examples include:

- Official company sources
- Official reports
- Primary announcements
- Direct interviews
- Reliable professional publications

## 7.9 Privacy & Data Minimization

Executive research should focus only on:

- Relevant
- Public
- Professional

information.

V1 should avoid unnecessary personal profiling.

## 7.10 Basic Audit Trail

Important findings should be traceable through:

**Finding → Evidence → Source → Analysis → Final Brief**

---

# 8. Final V1 Output

The main product output is:

# Strategic Meeting Intelligence Brief

The brief should contain:

1. **Executive Summary**
2. **Company Snapshot**
3. **Current Strategy & Direction**
4. **Important Projects & Client Cases**
5. **AI / Technology Activity**
6. **Target Executive Profile**
7. **Executive's Current Professional Priorities**
8. **Key Strategic Signals**
9. **Opportunity Areas for My Goal**
10. **Why I May Be Relevant**
11. **Recommended Meeting Topics**
12. **Smart Questions to Ask**
13. **Risks / Do Not Assume**
14. **Unknowns / Knowledge Gaps**
15. **Evidence & Sources**

The report should clearly distinguish:

**FACT / INFERENCE / RECOMMENDATION**

---

# 9. V1 Research Principle

V1 should not create a large encyclopedia about a company or executive.

Every important finding should help answer:

> **Why does this matter for my meeting?**

Research that does not improve meeting preparation should normally be excluded from the final brief.

---

# 10. Out of Scope for V1

To protect the three-week development limit, V1 does not require:

- Continuous company monitoring
- Automatic alerts
- CRM integration
- Automatic LinkedIn scraping
- Large-scale competitive intelligence
- Enterprise multi-user support
- Team collaboration
- Complex permissions
- Enterprise dashboards
- Large autonomous agent teams
- Fully automated outreach
- Automated negotiation
- Automated decision-making
- Deep personal profiling
- Large-scale data collection

These may be considered after V1.

---

# 11. V1 Completion Definition

V1 is complete when a user can provide:

**Company + Executive + Meeting Goal**

and the system can produce a useful:

**Strategic Meeting Intelligence Brief**

that:

- Contains relevant company intelligence
- Contains relevant executive intelligence
- Identifies important projects and strategic signals
- Produces meeting-specific strategic analysis
- Provides useful meeting topics and questions
- Connects important factual claims to evidence
- Separates Fact / Inference / Recommendation
- Handles uncertainty instead of inventing information
- Respects the defined public-information boundaries
- Can be used before a real meeting

V1 should be:

**Useful → Trustworthy → Explainable → Maintainable**

before additional features are added.
